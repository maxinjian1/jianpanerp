- 候选品牌：从母婴类里挑了 26 个欧美澳品牌，在 Amazon.com 搜品牌名，看首页有多少件是该品牌、最多评价数（评价数只是粗略读数）。
- 日本情况：① Amazon.co.jp 搜同一品牌名；② 网上搜「品牌名 + 日本 正規代理店 公式」各 1 次；③ 看品牌官网的经销商页。

- 没有查销量排名（BSR）、没有查乐天上的并行输入情况、没有查商标在日本的注册情况。

## 四、英文邮件（通用版）

**Subject:** Japan distribution inquiry (Rakuten / Amazon.co.jp / Yahoo! Shopping) – ANET Co., Ltd.

Hello,

My name is Ma Xinjian from ANET Co., Ltd. (アネット株式会社), a company registered in Japan. I am writing to ask whether 【Brand】 is open to working with a Japanese partner.

About us
- We run our own stores on Rakuten Ichiba, Amazon.co.jp and Yahoo! Shopping Japan.
- Our Rakuten store focuses on baby and kids products. Our best-selling line there is BANZ, an Australian brand of baby earmuffs and kids' sun protection.
- We handle Japanese product pages, customer service and after-sales in Japan.

Why we are contacting you
【Brand】 sells very well on Amazon in the US, and we could not find an official distributor or an official Rakuten store for it in Japan. Rakuten and Yahoo! Shopping require a Japanese legal entity to open a store, so they are hard for overseas brands to enter directly. We can list your products on our existing stores.

What we would like to ask
1. Do you already have a distributor or an exclusive partner in Japan?
2. Would you consider an authorized reseller or distributor for Japan, and can you issue a letter of authorization?
3. Wholesale pricing, minimum order quantity, lead time, and whether you can ship to Japan.
4. Any test reports or material documents you have for your products (some categories need them for import into Japan).

We would like to start with a small trial order of a few best-selling items and grow from the sales data.

Thank you for your time. I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
Ma Xinjian (馬 信健)
Administration Dept., ANET Co., Ltd. (アネット株式会社)
https://anetb.com
